How to play

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Shift runs — faster, but guards hear it.
  • Guards patrol fixed routes and watch a yellow cone. A wall or a crate blocks their sight.
  • Detection fills the meter over a guard's head. At the top they chase you — break line of sight and they search, then give up.
  • Dark floor (the blue-black tiles) hides you: detection fills three times slower there. The HUD says HIDDEN when you are in shadow.
  • Shards (cyan diamonds) are the job. Take them all and the exit turns green.
  • Cameras sweep back and forth on the last floor. Being seen by one alarms every guard.
  • P pauses, R restarts the floor, M mutes, H closes this help.
